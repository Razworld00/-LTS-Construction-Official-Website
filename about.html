About LTS Construction

Our Story

Built on Experience

LTS Construction (Pty) Ltd, registration number 2018/353263/07, is a KwaZulu-Natal-based main contractor founded in 2018. With over 45 years of leadership experience, we deliver reliable, cost-effective building solutions across residential, commercial and industrial sectors.

We provide turnkey construction services as main contractor – from groundworks and foundations through structural works, finishes and final handover. Our clients include property developers, commercial operators and private homeowners who value clear communication, programme control and quality workmanship.

Leadership

Director David Mouton brings more than 45 years of extensive industry experience. His leadership ensures strong project direction, rigorous quality control and programme-driven delivery on every contract the company undertakes.
